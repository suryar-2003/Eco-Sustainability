💧 Ways to reduce water waste

  • Shorter showers: a typical shower head uses roughly 8–10 L per minute, so each minute saved matters.
  • Fix leaks: a dripping tap or running toilet can waste tens of litres a day.
  • Turn the tap off while brushing teeth or scrubbing dishes.
  • Run washing machines and dishwashers only with full loads.
  • Water plants early morning or evening, and reuse rinse water for plants where safe.

⚡ Energy-saving tips

  • Switch off lights and fans when you leave a room.
  • Use LED bulbs; they use far less energy than old incandescent ones.
  • Set air conditioners around 24–26 °C and clean filters regularly.
  • Unplug chargers and devices that sit on standby; it adds up over a month.
  • Use natural light and air-dry clothes when the weather allows.

Sustainability & Ethics

Why these habits matter, and how EcoTrack treats your data.

💧 Why save water and electricity?

Fresh water is limited and treating and pumping it takes energy. Electricity in many places still comes partly from fossil fuels, so using less can mean fewer greenhouse gas emissions. Saving also lowers your bills.

🌍 Individual actions count

One household won’t solve climate change alone, and it’s honest to say so. But habits spread, they add up across millions of people, and they push shops, employers and governments to act too.

♻️ Responsible consumption

  • Buy only what you need; repair and reuse first.
  • Choose reusable bottles, bags and containers.
  • Cut food waste; growing and moving food uses water and energy.
  • Prefer efficient appliances when you do replace them.

🔒 Privacy & responsible data use

  • Minimal data: EcoTrack asks only for a date, an amount and an optional category. No name, email, location or account.
  • How it’s stored: entries live in your browser’s localStorage on this device. Clearing site data, using another browser or private mode means they won’t be there.
  • Never shared: there is no server, analytics or tracking. Your numbers are not sent or sold.
  • You’re in control: edit or delete any entry, or use “Reset all data” on the Dashboard.
  • Honest numbers: savings shown are based only on what you enter. Real impact depends on your local water and power sources.
